Our Story

Born by the Lee, built on good beans.

Café Depeche started as a three-table corner on Pope's Quay with a second-hand roaster and a stubborn belief that Cork deserved coffee worth slowing down for. A decade on, we still roast in-house every week and bake before the sun is properly up.

Everything is made with intent — from the single-origin filter we cup each morning to the sourdough we fold by hand. Pull up a stool by the window, watch the river go by, and stay as long as you like.

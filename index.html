Our Story

Born on the quay. Brewed every morning.

Café Depeche has been part of Cork life since 2003. We started on Lancaster Quay with a simple belief — that great coffee shouldn't be complicated or precious. Coffee for the masses, we called it. Over twenty years later, that hasn't changed.

The brunch menu changes with the seasons. The commitment to a good cup doesn't. We were proud to be named Oceanic Regional Winner at the Food Awards Ireland 2024, and we still make every dish like we're trying to win it again.
